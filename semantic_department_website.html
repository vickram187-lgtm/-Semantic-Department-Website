About the Department

Empowering future software architects, computational scientists, and technology innovators since 1998.

🎯 Our Vision

To evolve into a globally recognized center of excellence in Computer Science education and cutting-edge research, producing ethical engineers capable of solving complex societal problems.

🚀 Our Mission

  • Deliver robust foundational and modern technical curriculum.
  • Foster industry collaboration and interdisciplinary research culture.
  • Cultivate leadership qualities, entrepreneurship, and societal responsibility.

Department History

Established in 1998 with an initial intake of 60 undergraduate students, the Department of Computer Science & Engineering has grown exponentially over the last two decades. Today, it hosts over 800 students across undergraduate (B.Tech), postgraduate (M.Tech), and doctoral (Ph.D.) programs.

Our alumni work in leadership roles across top multinational tech giants, research labs, and pioneering tech startups globally.

State-of-the-Art Facilities
